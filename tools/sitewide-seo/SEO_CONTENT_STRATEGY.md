# Floss Fox organic search and editorial strategy

## Positioning

Floss Fox is a **friendly cross-stitch companion**, not just a DMC→Anchor chart. Product promise: organize thread, understand conversions, prepare projects, shop thoughtfully, calculate fabric and return to what matters. Pip provides warmth without hiding the actual tool names.

**Tone:** Helpful, cozy, factual. Clear and welcoming first; poetic where it supports meaning. No hype, artificial urgency, fabricated social proof or coercive engagement.

## Information architecture

- `/` — primary product and application hub, brand query and broad cross-stitch helper intent.
- `/tools.html` — crawlable directory of actually available tools; supports internal discovery.
- `/floss-conversion-guide.html` — educational, high-intent brand conversion topic; internal link to Find Floss.
- `/thread-inventory-guide.html` — practical floss organization and import guide; internal link to My Threads.
- `/cross-stitch-project-planning.html` — pre-stitch planning tutorial; links to fabric, inventory, projects and shopping.
- `/fabric.html` — original calculator landing page; strong task-specific title, description and crosslinks.
- `/about.html` — human story, source transparency and independent development context.
- `/privacy.html` — direct storage and privacy explanation, not a sales page.
- `/404.html` and `/offline.html` — helpful utility pages, noindex.

## Copy guardrails

- State DMC→Anchor and DMC→Sullivans matches are based on published charts and not guaranteed visual equivalents.
- Keep DMC BLANC and WHITE distinct and warn about metallic, variegated and specialty substitutions.
- A quantity absent in a spreadsheet is unknown, not 1 skein by default without review.
- Confirm product features on the live 4.0 site before marketing them. Particularly, **Premium photo OCR, scanner subscription validation and account status must not be promised as deployed.**
- Local browser storage is not a cloud-sync service and should not be described as such.
- Keep existing first-person About page human; SEO headings must not erase the author's story.
- The current privacy policy may require a separate accuracy update after new online features; this package only improves its search presentation.
- Avoid generic long-form content with no unique use case. New guide material is written as practical instruction tied to existing tools.

## Longer-term editorial backlog (not included in installer)

1. Real, user-tested walkthroughs for free collection import, with annotated original screenshots and accessible text.
2. Cross-stitch count and fabric calculation examples, including 14-count Aida vs over-two evenweave (verify formulas before publishing).
3. Thread-storage comparison using firsthand organizer photos and costs, with proper image rights and attribution.
4. Tested material on substituting near-identical shades; demonstrate differences under consistent lighting without fabricating quantitative color accuracy.
5. Helpful troubleshooting pages based on real support/tester questions rather than mass-generated pages.

Every new page should have a clear query intent, distinctive purpose, human review, internal links, an accurate title, and a plan to keep facts current.
