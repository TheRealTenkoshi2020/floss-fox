Pip's tips

Where are the stitch counts? Look on the pattern cover, the info sheet or the first chart page for something like "Stitches: 140 w × 100 h".

What does count mean? It's how many squares (Aida) or threads (evenweave/linen) fit in one inch. Higher count = smaller stitches = smaller finished design.

Why extra fabric? A margin of fabric gives the hoop something to hold now and the frame something to grip later.

What do the results mean? "Your finished design" is the stitched area. "Fabric to cut" adds your extra fabric on every side and rounds up to the next ¼ inch.

How this calculator works

Divide the number of stitches by the stitches per inch of your fabric. On Aida, stitches per inch equals the count; on evenweave or linen stitched over two threads, it's half the count (28-count over two = 14 stitches per inch). Then add the extra fabric twice, once for each side, to get the fabric to cut. Floss Fox rounds the fabric to cut up to the next ¼ inch, so rounding never leaves you short, and works out centimeters from that rounded inch value.
