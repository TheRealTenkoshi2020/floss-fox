How to organize and track your floss collection

Your thread collection can be a treasure box rather than a mystery. A little numbering and a simple inventory makes it easier to know what you own before starting a new piece.

Start with numbers, not just colors

Some floss shades look nearly identical under warm light. Keep each skein's brand and printed color number attached to its bobbin, bag or organizer. Avoid identifying an unlabelled skein by appearance alone when the project depends on a precise match.

Choose a storage system that fits your habits: numbered bobbins in compartments, skeins grouped by number, or project bags. The best system is one you can actually maintain.

What to record

  • Brand and color number: a DMC 310 is different from a similarly numbered thread in another brand.
  • Full skeins: count unopened or confidently complete skeins separately from partial ones.
  • Partial skeins: mark an opened skein without pretending its remaining length is exact.
  • Storage location: note which box, drawer, row or project bag holds the thread.
  • Restock note: keep a separate reminder for shades you want more of.

Bring in an existing list

If you already use a spreadsheet or another tracker, start with that work rather than retyping everything. In My Threads, choose “Import a list” to review compatible CSV, TSV, TXT, JSON or XLSX files. You can also paste a list. Verify the column for thread number, the original brand and whether a quantity means skeins or stitches before you confirm any changes.

Review duplicates and unexpected quantities. If your list records only which colors you own, leave counts unconfirmed until you check your collection.

Keep your inventory useful

  1. Add newly purchased skeins when they arrive, not when you merely mark them bought.
  2. Update amounts after using thread on a project.
  3. Check missing colors before shopping so you don't buy doubles.
  4. Export a backup periodically; browser storage is not automatic cloud sync.

A collection export is useful for a spreadsheet. Use the full backup in Settings to preserve projects, preferences and stitching sessions as well as your collection. Keep the downloaded file somewhere you can find it.

Ready to begin? Open My Thread Collection and choose the entry method that feels easiest.
