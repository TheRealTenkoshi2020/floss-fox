A gentle guide to floss color conversion

Your pattern calls for one brand, but your shop carries another. A conversion chart can help you find a starting point, without pretending every thread is a perfect match.

What a floss conversion chart does

Cross-stitch patterns commonly list thread colors using a brand and number, such as DMC. A conversion chart pairs those codes with suggested colors in another brand, such as Anchor or Sullivans. The number is a reference within that brand, not a universal shade code.

Floss Fox uses published DMC-to-Anchor and DMC-to-Sullivans chart mappings. A chart-listed pairing is not a promise that two skeins look identical. Some colors have no verified pairing in the source data.

How to choose a substitute

  1. Check the pattern key. Confirm the original brand, number and thread type.
  2. Look up chart-listed matches. Enter the DMC number in Find Floss and review any alternatives shown.
  3. Compare real thread in your lighting. Phone screens and printed swatches can differ from physical skeins; judge near the fabric you will use.
  4. Keep close shades together. If your design blends several similar colors, consider substituting the group carefully rather than swapping isolated shades.
  5. Record your decision. Keep the final brand and code with your project so shopping and restocking are less confusing.

Special colors deserve extra care

DMC BLANC and WHITE are distinct catalog codes. Preserve them separately when copying lists. Metallic, variegated and specialty threads may behave and reflect light differently from ordinary stranded cotton; do not assume a standard cotton conversion preserves the intended effect.

For shades without a verified mapping, a responsible result is “no confirmed chart match,” not an invented near-equivalent.

Compare a screen color with DMC swatches

The optional color tool in Settings compares a six-digit HEX color with DMC screen swatches using CIEDE2000 color distance. Its results are estimates, not approved thread equivalents or measurements of physical skeins. Published brand conversions remain separate. Compare real thread before choosing a substitute.

Suggest a better thread match

If you have compared actual skeins, open Help and choose “Suggest a better thread match.” You can copy a report with the brand, codes and your observations. It stays on your device until you choose where to send it; copying does not submit feedback or change the conversion tables.

What about price and availability?

A cheaper skein may save money, but swapping every color in a detailed design can affect the final appearance. Check your existing thread collection first, then use a shopping list for what is truly missing. Store inventory and prices can change, so verify them directly with the retailer.
