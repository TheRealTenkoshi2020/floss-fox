Privacy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Right now Floss Fox collects nothing. There are no accounts, analytics, ads, tracking scripts or cookies. The thread list you paste is processed entirely in your browser and never sent anywhere.

Saved projects. If you choose “Save project”, that project (thread list, quantities, Have/Check/Buy choices, notes) is stored in your own browser's local storage on this device only. It is never uploaded, and Floss Fox can't see it. It isn't synced between devices, and clearing site data, private browsing, or the browser itself can delete it, so use “Download backup” to keep a copy. Delete a project, or clear this site's data in your browser settings, to remove it.

Our hosting provider (e.g. GitHub Pages or Netlify) may keep standard server logs such as IP address and browser type for security and operations. That's handled under their own privacy policy.

Possible future changes

Store and affiliate links

Right now Floss Fox shows no store links. In the future, a "Where to buy your missing supplies" section may list a few retailers, but only ones that have approved Floss Fox and whose links have been checked by hand. Some of those links may be affiliate links, and the section says so right next to them.

Floss Fox itself has no tracking: no analytics, no ads, no pixels, no cookies. It never sends your thread list, projects, stash or notes to any store or network. Store links contain only a fixed address chosen in advance, never your colors, amounts or project names. Nothing is sent anywhere until you click a link.

When you do click one, your browser opens the retailer's site (sometimes via an affiliate network such as Awin). Like any website you visit, that site and network receive ordinary browser information (for example your IP address and browser type) and may set their own cookies so a purchase can be credited. Those sites are not controlled by Floss Fox; their own privacy policies apply. Floss Fox doesn't know their prices or stock.
