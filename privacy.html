Privacy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

The short version

Floss Fox has no accounts, no ads and no cookies. Your lists, projects and thread collection are saved in your own browser, on your device. Floss Fox never uploads your projects or thread collection. We don't run a server that receives them, so we can't see them.

What stays on your device

When you paste a list on Find My Floss, it's read right here in your browser.

If you tap Save project, the project is saved in this browser's storage on this device. That includes its name and notes, your colors, amounts and stitch counts, your Have enough / Need to buy / Not sure choices, the brand to buy, what you've marked as bought and checked in, Pip's estimate settings, and when the project was created and last changed.

Nothing is synced between browsers or devices. To move your work, use a backup (see below).

Your thread collection

My Thread Collection is saved the same way, in this browser. It holds the DMC colors you own, your full skein counts, partial skeins, restock marks and any storage locations you add (box, section and storage format).

Pip and your settings

We also remember a few small settings in this browser: whether Pip is shown, whether hints are on, which of Pip's tips you've closed, where you are in the tour, and your Fabric calculator settings. They stay on this device too.

Importing a pattern

When you use Import a pattern (PDF), your file is opened in your browser. PDF text is read on your device with a PDF reader that's built into Floss Fox. Only PDF files can be imported. We don't keep the file. If you create a project from it, the project keeps the colors and amounts you checked, plus the short legend lines they came from.

Working offline

So Floss Fox can work offline, your browser keeps a copy of our own files (pages, code and Pip's pictures). That copy holds app files only, never your lists.

Backups

Download a backup saves a file named like floss-fox-backup-2026-10-17.json wherever your browser saves downloads. It contains your projects, your thread collection and the small settings above (Pip, hints, tips and Fabric calculator). Demo data isn't included. We never receive a copy. Anyone who has the file can open it, so keep it somewhere you trust.

Before you restore a backup, we keep a safety copy of your current data in this browser so you can use Undo last import. If saved data ever can't be read, we set it aside in browser storage instead of deleting it.

Clearing browser data

Clearing this site's data (or browser data that includes it) removes your projects, thread collection, settings, safety copies and the offline copy from this browser. Private or incognito windows usually clear them when you close the window. Backup files you've downloaded aren't affected, and Restore from a backup brings your work back.

To remove a single project, open it and choose Delete this project.

Hosting

Floss Fox is a set of web pages hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open a page, your browser requests it from GitHub's servers, like any website. GitHub says it logs visitors' IP addresses for security. That's handled by GitHub under its own privacy statement. Apart from the simple visit count below, Floss Fox doesn't add any tracking of its own.

We count visits with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and doesn't store personal information, so we can see how many people use Floss Fox. Your threads, projects and pattern files are never sent.

Store links

Floss Fox works out your list first, on its own. Store links never change your list, your amounts or what you own. Nothing is sent to a store until you click a link.

Store links are fixed addresses chosen in advance. They never include your amounts, project names or notes. A link to a specific product shows the store which color you're looking at, just as browsing their site would.

When you click one, the store's site opens in a new tab. Like any website, it receives ordinary browser information (such as your IP address and browser type) and may set its own cookies. Those sites aren't run by Floss Fox, and their own privacy policies apply.

Right now, none of our store links earn a commission. If we add affiliate links later, they'll appear only in a clearly marked section, with this note right beside them: "Floss Fox may earn a commission if you buy through these links, at no extra cost to you." Those links may go through an affiliate network such as Awin, which may set its own cookies so a purchase can be credited.

Online AI

Pip's built-in help works on your device. Online AI answers and AI pattern scanning are turned off on Floss Fox right now, so nothing from Ask Pip or pattern import is sent anywhere.

If we ever turn online AI on, we'll update this page first. The feature will show you exactly what will be sent, and nothing leaves your device until you approve it.

Changes

When what Floss Fox saves or sends changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions or corrections? Open an issue on GitHub. Issues are public, so please don't include personal details.
