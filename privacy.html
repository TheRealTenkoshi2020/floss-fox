Privacy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Right now Floss Fox collects nothing. There are no accounts, analytics, ads, tracking scripts or cookies. The thread list you paste is processed entirely in your browser and never sent anywhere.

Our hosting provider (e.g. GitHub Pages or Netlify) may keep standard server logs such as IP address and browser type for security and operations. That's handled under their own privacy policy.

Possible future changes

I may add affiliate links to floss shops later. If you click one, that shop or its affiliate network (for example Amazon or Awin) may set a cookie so they can credit Floss Fox for a purchase. Those cookies follow the shop's own privacy policy. I'll update this page and label affiliate links before that happens.
