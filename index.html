What can I paste?
  • DMC numbers: 310, DMC 321 Red, plus Blanc, Ecru and B5200
  • Skein amounts: 310 x2, 310 ×2, 310x2, 310 (2) or 310 2 skeins
  • Don't know the amount? Write 310 x?. If you leave the amount off, Floss Fox counts 1 skein and marks it "assumed".
  • Not supported yet: other brands' numbers, PDFs and photos. Paste DMC numbers only.

About the brands

Sullivans (cotton alternative): numbers from Sullivans' official DMC chart; 100% cotton and the DMC number is printed on the label. Stitchers say it holds up well, though a few shades differ.

CXC (budget brand): sold mainly as full sets. Reviewers say it uses DMC-style numbers, but Floss Fox has no verified per-colour data or set contents, so it isn't offered per colour. Poly-cotton and a bit fluffier.

Metallic and variegated threads aren't substituted automatically. Colour swatches show the DMC colour only.

      How Floss Fox works

      1. 1
        Add your colors. Paste the DMC list from your pattern. Floss Fox shows what it recognized and anything it couldn't read.
      2. 2
        Check what you have. Save the colors as a project and tap Have enough, Need to buy or Not sure for each one.
      3. 3
        Get your shopping list. Open Shop my list in the store, or when you order online, then check each thread in when you have it.

      What the words mean

      Have enough
      You've checked and have enough for this pattern.
      Need to buy
      It goes on your shopping list.
      Not sure
      Check your stash later. It stays on your to-do list until you decide.
      Bought
      You paid for it, but it isn't counted until you check it in.
      Received
      You have it and entered how many skeins arrived.
      Ready to stitch
      Every color has enough thread, every amount is known, and every brand match is confirmed.

      About colors and brands

      Color swatches are screen approximations, not thread proofs. Other brands come from published conversion charts. When a chart gives more than one match, or the sources disagree, you choose. Compare real thread before swapping it into a stitched piece.
