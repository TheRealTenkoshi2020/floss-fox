Free stitching guides

Cross-stitch tools, all in one cozy place

Meet Pip, your friendly fox guide. Whether you are sorting skeins, preparing a new pattern or working out how much fabric to cut, start with the tool that helps today.

Choose what you need

Find floss and compare brands

Working from a DMC pattern? Look up chart-listed Anchor and Sullivans matches, review the limitations, and decide what to buy. Open Find Floss or read the floss conversion guide.

Organize your thread collection

Record DMC numbers, full and partial skeins, and where your threads live. Bring an existing list into your collection, then keep a backup. Open My Threads or read the inventory guide.

Plan a cross-stitch project

Save your pattern thread list, compare it with your stash, and gather missing skeins into a combined shopping list. Keep a stitching journal and update your progress when you choose. Open My Projects or follow the planning guide.

Calculate fabric size

Enter the design's stitch dimensions, fabric count and finishing margins to calculate a cutting size. Open Pip's fabric calculator.

Do I need an account?

The free core tools work without signing up. Projects and thread information are generally saved in the browser on your device, so export a backup if you want to protect or move your work. See privacy and storage details.

A note about color matches

Conversion tables suggest possible substitutes, not guaranteed visual equivalents. Dye lots, lighting and floss types matter. For large or important pieces, compare actual skeins before purchasing.

Bring your next stitching question to Floss Fox. Pip and your tools are waiting. There's no need to finish everything today.
